Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Apokria Tarot is an iOS app for tarot readings and reflection. This policy explains what stays on your device, what you may choose to share, and how to reach us.

Information you can enter

You can enter a question for a reading, write notes in your journal, and add optional reading profile details: a display name or nickname, preferred reading style, life areas, current focus, and additional context. The app also records the cards and deck in saved readings, your daily draws, and preferences such as your selected deck and copying options.

Where this information is stored

When you reach Final Spread, Apokria Tarot automatically saves the completed reading to your Journal on your device. If you abandon an incomplete reading, it is not automatically added to the Journal. Saved readings and journal notes are stored as files in the app’s storage on your device.

Daily Draw saves your draws separately from the Journal in the app’s local settings. Profile details, preferences, and any custom AI prompt are also stored in the app’s local settings on your device. Apokria Tarot does not provide an account or its own cloud synchronization.

Your device’s backup settings may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup managed by you and Apple. That is separate from an Apokria account or sync service.

Information we receive

The app does not automatically send your readings, journal notes, daily draws, profile details, AI prompt, or usage information to Apokria Tarot or an external server. We do not receive that information through the app. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use that information only for support.

Copying readings for AI

When you tap Copy for AI, the app puts structured reading text on the iOS clipboard. The text can include your question, card details, saved journal notes, and an optional AI interpretation prompt. Personal profile context is included only if you opt in with Include profile context under Settings → Copied Readings. If you previously opted in, that choice is retained until you change it.

Include AI prompt is on by default. Under Settings → Copied Readings, you can use Customize AI Prompt to save your own prompt on your device or use Reset to Default to return to the built-in prompt. Turning off Include AI prompt leaves the prompt out of copied readings and keeps your saved custom text.

Copying does not send the reading to us or to an AI service. If you paste it into another app, including a third-party AI app, that app receives what you paste and its own privacy practices apply.

Sharing readings

Share Reading opens the native iOS share sheet with card-only text. The text identifies the deck and spread or Daily Draw and lists the card details. It excludes questions, personal profile context, private journal notes, and AI instructions. You choose whether and where to share it. The destination’s own privacy practices apply.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Information saved in Apokria Tarot stays in the app’s local storage until you change or delete it, or delete the app. You can delete individual saved readings from the Journal, edit your reading profile and saved journal notes, choose what profile context is included in copied readings, and choose whether to copy a reading at all. The app does not offer a way to delete individual daily draws.

Deleting the app through iOS removes its data from that device. Existing device backups, text you have shared with other apps, and support emails are handled separately. You can manage device backups in iOS settings. Support emails may remain in our mailbox until deleted; we delete them on request. Because the app does not send us its local data, we cannot access or delete it for you.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy if the app’s practices change. We will post the revised policy here with a new effective date. For privacy questions or to request deletion of a support email, contact apokriatarot@gmail.com.
