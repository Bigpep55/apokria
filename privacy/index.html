Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Apokria Tarot is an iOS app for tarot readings and reflection. This policy explains what stays on your device, what you may choose to share, and how to reach us.

Information you can enter

You can enter a question for a reading, write notes in your journal, and add optional reading profile details: a display name or nickname, preferred reading style, life areas, current focus, and additional context. The app also records the cards and deck in saved readings, your daily draws, and preferences such as your selected deck and copying options.

Where this information is stored

Saved readings and journal notes are stored as files in the app’s storage on your device. Daily draws, profile details, and preferences are stored in the app’s local settings on your device. Apokria Tarot does not provide an account or its own cloud synchronization.

Your device’s backup settings may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup managed by you and Apple. That is separate from an Apokria account or sync service.

Information we receive

The current app does not send your readings, daily draws, profile details, or usage information to Apokria Tarot or an external server. We do not receive that information through the app. The app does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use it to respond to your request.

Copying and sharing readings

When you tap Copy Reading, the app puts formatted reading text on your device’s clipboard. That text can include your question, card details, and journal notes. A setting lets you choose whether to include optional profile context; a separate setting controls whether to include a suggested AI prompt. You can review these settings before copying.

Copying does not send the reading to us or to an AI service. If you paste it into another app, including a third-party AI app, that app receives what you paste and its own privacy practices apply. Consider what personal details you include before sharing.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Information saved in Apokria Tarot stays in the app’s local storage until you change it or delete the app. You can edit your reading profile and saved journal notes, choose what profile context is included in copied readings, and choose whether to copy a reading at all. The current app does not offer a way to delete individual saved readings or daily draws.

Deleting the app through iOS removes its data from that device. Existing device backups, text you have shared with other apps, and support emails are handled separately. You can manage device backups in iOS settings and ask us about a support email at the address below. Because the app does not send us its local data, we cannot access or delete it for you.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy if the app’s practices change. We will post the revised policy here with a new effective date. For privacy questions or requests about information you sent to support, email apokriatarot@gmail.com.
